## Objective

Prove the G4 adapter slice: cache hit/miss/eviction/TTL/invalidation/concurrency and messaging publish/consume/groups/DLQ/idempotency/reconnect/TLS via Testcontainers.

## Scope

### In Scope

- `test/integration/cache/cache_test.go`: Otter+Valkey hybrid vs Testcontainers Valkey (hit/miss/eviction/TTL/invalidation/concurrency, L1<1ms/L2<5ms benchmarks).
- `test/integration/messaging/messaging_test.go`: NATS fanout/groups, Redpanda-path via in-memory broker fake + Valkey receipts, webhook isolation, reconnect/TLS-shape tests.
- Docker-skip guards (`SkipIfNoDocker`) so unit CI stays green without Docker.

### Out of Scope

- Production adapter changes (E08-T01..T05 own), provider suites (E10-T06), contract/perf/chaos (E16).
