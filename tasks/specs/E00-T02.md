## Objective

One `Makefile` entry point for build/test/lint/dev workflows so every later
epic and CI uses the same definition of "done".

## Scope

### In Scope

- `Makefile` with all targets named by `tasks/epics/E00-foundation.md#E00-T02`
  and a self-describing `make help`.

### Out of Scope

- Script bodies behind the targets (E00-T04 dev scripts, E00-T07
  build/test/generate/release/security scripts); the Makefile references their
  canonical paths and degrades gracefully until they exist.
- Full CI matrix (E17 extends the E00-T05 workflow).

## Normative Inputs

- `tasks/epics/E00-foundation.md#E00-T02` for the exact target list.
- `SPEC.md §15` (trackable phases) and `SPEC.md §12` (compose variants) for
  `dev-*`/`docker-build` targets.
- `tasks/SDD.md` for evidence and completion rules.

## Interfaces and Data

The `Makefile` is the interface: stable target names are the contract CI and
agents depend on. Target semantics (flags, output dirs) are documented inline
in `make help` lines.

## Invariants and Failure Semantics

- `set`-style safety: recipes fail fast (`.SHELLFLAGS`/prefixes); a failing
  recipe MUST fail the target (no swallowed errors).
- `bin/` output is gitignored (E00-T01 `.gitignore` covers it).
- Targets that need tools absent from the sandbox (Go, golangci-lint) fail
  with the tool's own "command not found", never with a Makefile syntax error.

## Change Surface

**Owns:** `Makefile`.  
**Coordinates:** script paths owned by E00-T04/E00-T07 (referenced, not created).  
**Must Not Touch:** Go sources, task statuses other than E00-T02, Git remotes.
