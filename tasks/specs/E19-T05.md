## Objective

Install the standalone `github.com/google/pprof` CLI (`v0.0.0-20261014150452-c3b4e57cab2c`) and `graphviz` (`dot`) in `scripts/dev/setup.sh`, add `make bench-profile` and `make pgo-profile` automation in `Makefile` / `scripts/test/bench-profile.sh` to generate CPU/heap/mutex/block profiles and SVG flamegraphs from ledger hot-path benchmarks, and produce validated `cmd/*/default.pgo` profiles for Go 1.27 Profile-Guided Optimization (`-pgo=auto`) release builds.

## Scope

### In Scope

- `scripts/dev/setup.sh`: add `graphviz` to `PIXI_PACKAGES` and `"pprof:github.com/google/pprof@v0.0.0-20261014150452-c3b4e57cab2c"` to `GO_TOOLS`.
- `scripts/test/bench-profile.sh` & `Makefile` (`bench-profile` and `pgo-profile` targets):
  - Execute hot-path benchmarks with `-cpuprofile`, `-memprofile`, `-mutexprofile`, `-blockprofile`.
  - Generate top cumulative reports (`pprof -top -cum`) and SVG callgraphs/flamegraphs (`pprof -svg`).
  - Merge CPU profiles (`pprof -proto`) into `cmd/{rest-api,grpc-api,graphql-api,cron,consumer}/default.pgo`.
- `scripts/build/build.sh` & `Makefile` (`build-all`): ensure `-pgo=auto` is enabled for all 5 service binaries.

### Out of Scope

- Continuous push profiling (`E09.2-T04`) and `docker-compose.observability.yml` Pyroscope server (`E15-T07`).
