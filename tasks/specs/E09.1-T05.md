## Objective

Prove the E09.1 capabilities compose (v2 lifecycle, authenticated UI matrix, distributed egress quota, single-flight collapse) with fake auth/provider seams and real Testcontainers Valkey, as the G4 slice that unblocks E10/E14 consumers.

## Scope

### In Scope

- `test/integration/scheduler-egress/...`: scheduler lifecycle, UI session/route matrix (fake authz/audit), egress burst against real Testcontainers Valkey, retry-permit composition, and coalescing cancellation/scope behavior.
- `docs/architecture/README.md`: append index entries for ADR-022…ADR-024 after the three ADRs are created; no other ADR history is rewritten.
- Static guards: no v1 import, only E08-T07's single Lua script, and E11 has no route to the admin router.
- Fake auth/provider dependencies and zero vendor credentials; Testcontainers Valkey is real. E14 durable-inbox binding remains downstream and is not implemented here.

### Out of Scope

- E14 job logic and consumer groups (E14-T02…T04 own).
- Provider adapters (E10-T01…T05 own; they consume the limiter/coalescer contracts).
- Public API/observability hardening (E11/E15 own).
