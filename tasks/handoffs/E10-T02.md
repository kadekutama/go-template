## Decisions and Assumptions

- `QuotedAt` is the platform observation instant; the vendor `timestamp` is a
  sanity bound (`fx.max_quote_age_sec`) rather than the stored quote time.
- `fx.api_key_env` names the environment variable; `config.FXConfig.APIKey`
  resolves it through an injected lookup so no secret is stored in `Config`.
- `config/egress/fx.yaml` ships the published free-plan quota (100 / 720h);
  production overlays must raise it for the purchased plan.
- Stale rates are returned with `nil` error and remain visibly stale;
  `service.Convert` still rejects them with `FX_RATE_STALE`.
- Egress order: breaker-open pre-check → `Coalescer.Do` → retry loop →
  `Egress.Wait` → breaker-wrapped single HTTP attempt → `httpclient.Doer`.
- The adapter-local `RateCache` and `Coalescer` seams are task-owned interfaces;
  their doubles are generated `test/mock/fx` mocks. `Egress` consumes the shared
  `internal/infrastructure/egress.Waiter`, doubled by `test/mock/egress`.
- `json.Number` was chosen over `json.RawMessage`: it accepts a bare number or a
  valid numeric string, rejects non-numeric strings at decode, and preserves the
  literal spelling. `math/big.Rat` remains because it reduces before the
  `int64` range check; the strict regex covers the grammar `SetString` would
  otherwise accept (fractions and exponents).
